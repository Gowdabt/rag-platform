First-week onboarding

Your laptop ships to your home address. If it has not arrived by day 2, email ithelp@acme.test.

Day 1 checklist

  • Join Slack workspace acme-hq and the channel #new-hires
  • Complete Okta MFA enrollment (authenticator app, not SMS)
  • Meet your onboarding buddy. Default buddy for engineering is Alex Chen.

The new-hire orientation call is every Monday at 09:30 IST / 21:00 PT.
