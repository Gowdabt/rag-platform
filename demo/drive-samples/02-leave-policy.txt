ACME LEAVE POLICY
=================

Parental leave: 16 weeks paid for the primary caregiver, 6 weeks paid for the secondary caregiver. Leave must start within 12 months of birth or adoption.

Bereavement: 5 paid days for immediate family.

Jury duty: paid for the duration of service, with a court summons on file.

Who to notify: email leave@acme.test at least 30 days before planned parental leave.
