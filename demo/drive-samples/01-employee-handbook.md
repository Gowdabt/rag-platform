# Acme Employee Handbook

Last updated: 12 September 2026

## Time off

Full-time employees receive **18 PTO days** each calendar year. Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls into the next year. Sick leave is separate: **8 days**.

## Work model

Acme is hybrid. Employees are in-office Tuesday through Thursday. **Fridays are remote by default.** Mondays are optional office days.
