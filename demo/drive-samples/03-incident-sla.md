# Production incident SLA

| Severity | First response | Customer update | Restore target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 — full outage | 15 minutes | every 30 minutes | 2 hours |
| P2 — major degradation | 30 minutes | hourly | 8 hours |
| P3 — minor issue | 4 hours | daily | 3 business days |

On-call rotation is owned by **Site Reliability**. Page `#incident-war-room` on Slack for P1.

Do not post customer data in the public channel. Use the incident ticket in Linear (`INC-xxxx`).
